HEALTHCARE KNOWLEDGE RULES

Rule 1:
IF patient has Fever AND Chills AND Headache
THEN Malaria may be considered.

Rule 2:
IF patient has High Fever AND Joint Pain AND Skin Rash
THEN Dengue may be considered.

Rule 3:
IF patient has Persistent Cough AND Night Sweats AND Weight Loss
THEN Tuberculosis may be considered.

Rule 4:
IF patient has Fever AND Cough AND Fatigue
THEN Influenza may be considered.

Rule 5:
IF patient has Wheezing AND Shortness of Breath
THEN Asthma may be considered.

Rule 6:
IF patient has Excessive Thirst AND Frequent Urination
THEN Diabetes may be considered.

Rule 7:
IF patient has Headache AND Dizziness AND Blurred Vision
THEN Hypertension may be considered.

Rule 8:
IF patient has Cough AND Fever AND Chest Pain
THEN Pneumonia may be considered.

Rule 9:
IF patient has Diarrhea AND Vomiting AND Abdominal Pain
THEN Gastroenteritis may be considered.

Rule 10:
IF patient has Severe Headache AND Nausea
AND Sensitivity to Light
THEN Migraine may be considered.
